01 — The belief

In voice, there is no undo. So every word is load-bearing. I design the error path first — clarify once, rephrase, then hand off with context — because a bot is only as trustworthy as its worst moment.

02 — How I design conversations

Four rules,
played out live.

Scroll. Each principle runs as a real exchange from a shipped or speculative project.

01 / Failed UPI Debit

Diagnose first, talk second.

Reassuring someone before you’ve checked the ledger isn’t kindness. It’s a guess. My dispute engine runs five ledger checks before the conversation makes a single promise, so tickets for money that already came back can’t get raised in the first place.

Banking AI · Tier I Indian bank

02 / Dottie for IndiGo

Clarify once. Rephrase. Hand off.

When the bot doesn’t understand, it has exactly three moves, in this order. It never loops, never asks the same question twice, and never leaves anyone stuck. The handoff carries everything the user already said.

Voice AI · Speculative

03 / Beyond Touchscreens · ICICI

Words are the interface.

On a phone call there’s no screen to check and nothing to re-read. Every response has to tell the user what happened, what state they’re in now, and what happens next. The confirmation prompt is how the bot shows it understood.

Production · 70+ live flows

04 / Dottie for IndiGo

Personality carries the relationship.

A bot without personality is just a menu with a voice. The brand comes through in how the bot handles things going slightly wrong, and in a shortlist of three flights instead of a list of thirty.

Brand voice · Decision architecture

05 — The craft

What I bring to the table.

01

Conversation architecture

Happy paths, alternative paths, error paths. Flows that bend when people change their minds and pick up where they left off.

02

Voice UX & prompts

Designing for ASR misfires, noisy streets, code-switching and Indian English. “Tomorrow morning” means 7–11 AM.

03

Fallback & escalation logic

Confidence thresholds set by how risky each flow is. Warm handoffs that keep context. Decision boundaries for when to act, when to verify and when to stop.

04

NLU intents & entities

Intent hierarchies, annotation rules and training data, worked out with ML engineers. Edge cases go back in as training data.

05

Microcopy & persona

Brand voice turned into the exact words a bot uses. Professional, empathetic and precise, with everything the user needs in two sentences.

06

Compliance as design input

Making the case for design decisions in terms legal, brand and risk teams can evaluate.
